Historical report
1954
  • Cyanide poisoning
  • Poisoned apple
  • Unresolved truth
Death / discovery Found dead on 8 June 1954; his death is usually dated 7 June 1954.
Place His home in Wilmslow, Cheshire, England.
Official record Cyanide poisoning; inquest verdict: suicide.
Poisoned apple The most famous story, but the apple itself was not proven as the direct cause.
Accident theory Some accounts suggest accidental cyanide exposure during experiments, but the official record did not adopt that explanation.
Historical pressure Before his death, he had been prosecuted for homosexuality, lost security clearance, and undergone hormonal treatment.

The person behind the archive

He was a mathematician who changed modern computing, and a person deeply harmed by the laws and prejudices of his time.
